# Unique Requirements

## Technical Skills

*Hadoop, Spark, or Databricks.
*cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
*MLOps practices and model deployment pipelines.
*Background in natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, or deep learning.

## Programming Languages
* Python or R
*  C,C++, Java,

## Frameworks / Libraries

* pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch
* Big Data frameworks

## Tools & Technologies

* Tableau, Power BI, matplotlib, seaborn

## Platforms / Operating Systems

*Linux (Ubuntu, CentOS, or RHEL)

## Domain Knowledge

* finance, healthcare, e-commerce, etc.

## Responsibilities

* Collect, preprocess, and analyze structured and unstructured data from multiple sources.
* Develop and implement machine learning models, predictive algorithms, and statistical analyses.
* Design and run experiments to validate hypotheses and measure outcomes.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business problems into analytical solutions..
* Build scalable, production-ready models and monitor their performance over time.
* Ensure data integrity, quality, and compliance with company standards and policies.
* Stay up to date with emerging trends, tools, and techniques in data science and AI.

## Qualifications

* Master’s or Ph.D. in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related field.

## Preferred / Nice-to-Have Skills

* Programming Languages: Proficiency in Python or R for data modeling, alongside SQL for database management and querying.Core Libraries:      Hands-on experience with pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn for data manipulation and basic machine learning.
* Math and Statistics: Solid understanding of probability distributions, hypothesis testing, and linear algebra to interpret model performance.* Data Wrangling: Ability to clean, transform, and handle missing or outlier data.
* Machine Learning Fundamentals: Knowledge of supervised and unsupervised learning algorithms (regression, decision trees, clustering).
* Communication: Strong soft skills to translate complex data findings into actionable insights for non-technical stakeholders

## Soft Skills

* Data Visualization Tools: Proficiency in business intelligence tools like Tableau or Power BI for building dashboards.
* Version Control: Working knowledge of Git and GitHub for collaborative coding and workflow tracking.
* AI & MLOps Integration: Understanding of Large Language Model operations (LLMOps) or deploying real-time models into production.
* Domain Acumen: Specific industry knowledge relating to the product or business sector you support.
