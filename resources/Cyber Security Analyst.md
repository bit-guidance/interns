## Technical Skills

* Strong understanding of network protocols,  and cloud environments (Azure, AWS).
 *Proficiency with security tools such as SIEM, EDR, IDS/IPS, firewalls, and vulnerability scanners.
 *Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
 *SIEM: Splunk / Microsoft Sentinel / QRadar / ELK
*

## Responsibilities

* Monitor security systems and tools for alerts, anomalies, and potential security incidents.
 *Perform in-depth analysis of security events, logs, and network traffic to identify and investigate threats.
 **Respond to security incidents, including incident containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident analysis.
 *Conduct vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to identify weaknesses in our systems and applications.
 *Develop and implement security controls, policies, and procedures to enhance our security posture.
 *Collaborate with IT operations and development teams to integrate security best practices into our infrastructure and software development lifecycle.
 *Stay up-to-date with the latest cybersecurity threats, trends, and technologies.
 *Assist in security awareness training for employees.
 *Maintain documentation of security incidents, analyses, and configurations.
 *Participate in security audits and compliance initiatives.


## Qualifications

*Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, or a related field.
 
*2+ years of experience in a cybersecurity role, preferably within a corporate environment.
*

## Preferred / Nice-to-Have Skills

*Certifications such as CompTIA Security+, CEH, CySA+, 
*Ability to work independently and as part of a team in a fast-paced environment.
*

## Soft Skills

*Strong analytical and problem-solving ability

* Clear communication with technical & business stakeholders

* Ability to prioritize and work in high-pressure environments

* Experience working with global/onshore-offshore teams
