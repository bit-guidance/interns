## Technical Skills

*Strong analog/digital/mixed-signal/RF design knowledge (op-amps, filters, ADC/DAC, power domains, antenna design, impedance matching, RF PCB layout)
*Proficiency with lab equipment (oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, spectrum analyzers, VNAs, RF generators)
*Embedded hardware bring-up on TI/NXP/STM32 or equivalent platforms
*Experience with CAN, I2C, SPI, UART, RS-232/485, Ethernet, USB
*Exposure to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GNSS, LTE, LoRa, NB-IoT
*Schematic/PCB tools (OrCAD/Allegro, Altium)
*Power conversion/protection circuits (DC-DC, LDO, ESD/surge protection)
